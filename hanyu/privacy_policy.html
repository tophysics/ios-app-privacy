Privacy Policy

Hanyu — Chinese Learning App
Bundle ID: com.hongyue.use-hanyu-1.hanyu
Effective Date: May 27, 2026

1. Overview

Hanyu is an offline-first Chinese learning app. We believe your learning data belongs to you alone. This app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data with third parties.

Core Principle: All your learning progress, settings, and activity data stay on your device. Zero third-party SDKs, zero analytics, zero trackers, zero ads.

2. Data We Collect

Minimal Only data necessary for learning features, all stored on your device:

Data TypePurposeStorageShared?
Learning progressSpaced repetition & trackingLocal SwiftDataNo
User preferencesApp customizationLocal SwiftDataNo
Mistake recordsMistake review featureLocal SwiftDataNo
Study statisticsProgress dashboard & chartsLocal SwiftDataNo
Achievement recordsMilestone trackingLocal SwiftDataNo
Purchase statusMembership verificationiOS Keychain (device-only)No

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

None The following is never collected:

  • Personal identity (name, email, phone, age, gender)
  • Device identifiers (IDFA, IDFV, advertising IDs)
  • Location data (GPS, IP geolocation)
  • Network activity or browsing history
  • Biometric data (face, fingerprint, health)
  • Photos, camera, microphone, contacts
  • Analytics, crash logs, behavioral telemetry

4. Network & Third-Party Services

Zero network connectivity. No HTTP requests, API calls, or data transmissions. All content bundled locally. Audio via built-in AVSpeechSynthesizer.
  • No analytics SDKs — No Firebase, Mixpanel, etc.
  • No ad SDKs — No advertising frameworks
  • No crash reporting — No data sent to monitoring services
  • No social login — No Facebook, Google auth
  • No third-party dependencies — Only Apple native frameworks

5. iCloud Backup (Optional)

User-initiated Optional iCloud backup for cross-device continuity:

  • How: Tap "Backup to iCloud" → data written to your iCloud container (iCloud.com.hanyu.app).
  • Not automatic: Backup/restore are manual operations.
  • Your data stays yours: iCloud container tied to your Apple ID. No one else can access it.
  • Backed up: All learning progress, preferences, mistakes, statistics, achievements.
  • Not backed up: Purchase status (Keychain, device-only).

6. In-App Purchases

Purchases processed via Apple StoreKit. Payment handled entirely by Apple — we receive no payment info. Purchase status stored locally in Keychain with kSecAttrAccessibleAfterFirstUnlockThisDeviceOnly.

7. Permissions

Only one optional permission:

PermissionPurposeRequired?
NotificationsDaily study remindersOptional — only if you enable "Review Reminders"

No other permissions: Location, Camera, Photos, Contacts, Microphone, Health, Calendar are never accessed.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

All data stored locally. Delete by:

  • Deleting the app (removes all SwiftData)
  • Disabling iCloud Drive for this app in iOS Settings

No remote server exists to contact for deletion.

9. Children's Privacy

Suitable for all ages. No data collected from children or adults. No chat, social features, or third-party content exposure. In-app purchases require Apple ID auth controlled by parent/guardian.

10. Changes to This Policy

If data practices change, this policy will be updated with the new effective date. Given offline-first architecture, future changes would be minimal and transparent.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy?

Developer: Hongyue
Email: privacy@hanyu-app.com
App Store: Hanyu — Learn Chinese

1. 概要

Hanyuはオフライン優先の中国語学習アプリです。学習データはあなただけのものです。個人データの収集・送信・共有は一切ありません。

基本原則：全データは端末上に留まります。第三者SDKゼロ、分析ゼロ、トラッカーゼロ、広告ゼロ。

2. 収集データ

最小限 学習機能に必要なデータのみ、全て端末内保存：

データ目的保存共有?

3. 収集しないデータ

なし 一切収集しません：

  • 個人情報（名前・メール・電話・年齢・性別）
  • 端末識別子（IDFA・IDFV）
  • 位置情報（GPS・IP）
  • ネットワーク活動
  • 生体認証データ
  • 写真・カメラ・マイク・連絡先
  • 分析・クラッシュログ・テレメトリ

4. ネットワーク・第三者

ネットワーク接続ゼロ。HTTP・API通信なし。コンテンツは全て端末内。音声はAVSpeechSynthesizerで生成。
  • 分析SDKなし
  • 広告SDKなし
  • クラッシュ報告なし
  • ソーシャルログインなし
  • 第三者依存なし — Appleネイティブのみ

5. iCloudバックアップ（任意）

ユーザー手動 任意のiCloudバックアップ：

  • 方法："バックアップ"タップ→iCloudコンテナに保存
  • 自動なし：手動操作のみ
  • あなたのデータ：コンテナはApple IDに紐付け
  • バックアップ：進捗・設定・誤答・統計・達成
  • 非バックアップ：購入状態（Keychain）

6. アプリ内購入

Apple StoreKit経由。支払いはApple処理—支払い情報受領なし。状態はKeychainに端末内保存。

全データ端末内。削除方法：

  • 設定でiCloud Drive無効化

リモートサーバーなし。

全年齢対象。データ収集なし。チャット・ソーシャルなし。購入はApple ID認証（親管理）。
